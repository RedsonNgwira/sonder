🌐 Sonder

Self-hosted social simulation engine

Why I built this

I've always found social situations hard to read — the subtext, the history between people, the unspoken rules of a room. I wanted a way to practice, to understand, to step inside a scene and feel what it's like before facing it in real life.

Sonder is that tool. You describe a scene. AI agents with real personalities, real grievances, and real relationships spawn inside it and start living. You can watch, participate, or ask the narrator what's really going on beneath the surface.

It's free, self-hosted, and built to feel alive — not like a toy.
